# Magma inspiration

Research and creative-direction references for Magma's living archive and long-term physical social club in CDMX. These materials inform the design conversation; they are not approved visual specifications or additions to the product roadmap.

## Into the Night

- [Designer brief and highlighted quote bank](docs/inspiration/into-the-night-designer-brief.md)
- [Shareable PDF with the photographed source appendix](output/pdf/magma-into-the-night-designer-brief.pdf)

Added 15 October 2026. Covers the 38 supplied photographs, IMG_8857.jpeg through IMG_8894.jpeg, from *Into the Night: Cabarets and Clubs in Modern Art*. Includes thematic synthesis, 37 selected highlighted quotations with page/photo references, visual observations, handwritten marginalia, critical cautions, and a source register accounting for every photo.

Core connections: gathering as creative practice; the club and publication as extensions of one another; personal nostalgia alongside experimentation; a shared environment spanning objects and disciplines; Café de Nadie and the Carpa Amaro as local precedents; belonging without romanticising historical exclusion.

Working Magma concept: a living archive where different people's memories meet, and new ones are made. Visual identity remains open for exploration with the designer.
